Vulnerability risk and compliance

At IBM Research: machine learning that measures and reduces security risk across hybrid cloud, from grouping servers for patching and scoring vulnerabilities, to prioritizing them the way attackers would and mapping regulations to controls. Alongside it, representing IBM in the CVSS SIG, the group behind the vulnerability scoring standard.

  1. 2022
    Patent · IBM · Preprints Prioritization of attack techniques against an organization

    With preprints on prioritizing vulnerabilities by how attackers would use them, and on linking vulnerabilities to attack techniques.

  2. 2022
    Patent · IBM AI-assisted compliance mapping

    One of a series of compliance patents, alongside compliance process risk assessment and compliance content generation.

  3. 2021
    Paper · IEEE Cloud · Product NL2Vul: natural language to standard vulnerability scores

    CVSS scores from vulnerability descriptions, part of a vulnerability management service from IBM Research and X-Force Red.

  4. 2019
    Product · IBM Natural language to risk scores for security benchmarks

    Deep learning that standardizes risk across security processes, applied in IBM Cloud Pak for Multicloud Management.

  5. 2019
    Paper · IEEE Cloud Cognitive Compliance: analyze, monitor and enforce compliance in the cloud

    Machine learning that maps regulations such as HIPAA and PCI-DSS to the controls of a managed cloud.

  6. 2017

  7. 2017
