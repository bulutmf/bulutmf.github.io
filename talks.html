Long

Fatih Bulut is a Principal Applied Scientist at Microsoft, where he builds AI systems that make security operations autonomous. He trained Microsoft's first security-specific language model, SecEncoder, which shipped into Defender Experts for XDR and led to the company's first AI-driven threat actor discovery. He helped prototype Microsoft Security Copilot, shipped the Threat Intelligence Agent in Microsoft Perception to general availability, and now leads autonomous purple teaming, building agentic systems that find detection gaps before attackers do. Before Microsoft, he was a Senior Technical Staff Member at IBM Research, where his machine learning work on vulnerability prioritization, compliance policy management and hybrid cloud security shipped into X-Force Red and Cloud Pak products, and he represented IBM in the CVSS SIG. He holds 48+ patents, has 40+ publications, and teaches graduate courses at Columbia University.
